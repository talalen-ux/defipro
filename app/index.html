How it works

TradFi's largest market, rebuilt as four contracts.

Repo is ~$4–5T a day of instant liquidity against high-quality collateral. Tokenized treasuries have the collateral but not the market — redemption queues of days to months break composability. Meridian closes the gap.

01 · LEND

Deposit cash, earn the curve

Supply USDC to a term pool and hold pro-rata shares. Rates are utilization-priced and locked per repo, so your yield is paid by real borrowers — not emissions. Idle cash auto-parks in an external ERC-4626 venue and is recalled on demand.

02 · FINANCE

Post RWAs, draw cash in one block

Desks post tokenized T-bills or credit and borrow against them at up to a 98% advance rate, fixed for the term. Roll nightly or run 90 days — no more waiting out an issuer's redemption window.

03 · SETTLE

Margin-maintained, loss-isolated

Positions are marked against live price feeds. Breach maintenance margin or blow through maturity and anyone can liquidate at a small discount. Shortfalls stay inside the pool that underwrote them — never socialized across terms.

The benchmark

Every trade prints MOR. The market makes the rate.

Each action that moves a pool's utilization pushes its marginal rate to an on-chain accumulator — the same integral technique as a price TWAP. The overnight print is MOR; its time-weighted average is manipulation-resistant in proportion to the window, because distorting it means holding real capital at a distorted rate for a meaningful fraction of that window. Rate models get swapped by governance; the benchmark survives them. That's the moat: products price against benchmarks for decades.

Built on existing rails

No invented money. No invented venues.

Cash is canonical USDC. Idle liquidity earns in already-deployed ERC-4626 vaults. Risk primitives are OpenZeppelin's. Wallets connect over the standards you already use.
